Biscuit

ingredients:
- 2 cups (240g)
- Self-Rising Flour (Ensure it self-rising, as this contains the necessary baking powder and salt)
- 1 ½ cups (360ml) Heavy Whipping Cream (Pouring cream)

Instructions

1. Peheat & Prep: Heat your oven to 230°C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Mix: Pour the flour into a medium bowl. Add the heavy cream and stir gently with a wooden spoon or spatula just until the flour is moistened and a sticky dough forms. Do not overmix, or the biscuits will turn out tough.
3. Scoop: Use a large spoon or an ice cream scoop to drop dollops of dough onto your prepared baking tray, leaving a few centimeters between each.Bake: Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the tops are beautifully golden brown.
