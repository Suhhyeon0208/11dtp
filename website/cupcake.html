Cupcake

Ingredients
- Self-raising flour: 1 ½
- cupsCaster sugar: ¾
- cupMelted butter: 125g (cooled slightly)
- Eggs: 2 large eggs


Instructions


1. Preheat and Prep: Heat your oven to 180°C and line a 12-hole muffin tray with cupcake liners.
2. Mix Ingredients: Whisk the flour and sugar together in a large bowl. Pour in the melted butter and cracked eggs. Whisk gently just until smooth and no flour clumps remain.
3. Bake: Divide the batter into the 12 cases. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes until they are lightly golden and spring back when touched in the middle.
4. Cool: Let them cool completely before eating or icing
