Brownie

ingredients:
- 200g unsalted butter(melted)
- 1 cup white or brown sugar
- 2 large eggs1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup all-purpose (plain) flour
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder (sifted)
- Pinch of salt
- 1 cup chocolate chips or chunks (optional)

Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C and line an 20cm square baking pan with parchment paper.
2. Melt the butter in a heatproof bowl, then stir in the sugar and vanilla extract until smooth.
3. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking well after each addition until the mixture looks glossy and thick.
4. Fold in the dry ingredients (flour, cocoa powder, and salt) gently until just combined.
5. Stir in the chocolate chips if you are using them.Pour the batter into your prepared baking pan and smooth the top.
6. Bake for 24 to 28 minutes. Bake for 24 minutes for a gooey center, or 28 minutes for a firmer, fudgy texture.
7. Cool completely in the pan for at least 20 minutes before slicing into squares.
