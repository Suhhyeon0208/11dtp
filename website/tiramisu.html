Tiramisu

ingredients:
- 6 large egg yolks
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar (caster or superfine preferred)
- 1¼ cups (285g) mascarpone cheese, room temperature
- 1¾ cups (420ml) heavy whipping cream, cold
- 1½ cups (360ml) cold espresso or very strong coffee
- ½ cup (120ml) coffee liqueur
- 30 Italian ladyfingers

Instructions

1. Whisk the egg yolks and sugar continuously over the heat for about 10 minutes. The mixture will become thick and pale lemon-coloured, reaching a safe temperature of 74°C.
2. add the Mascarpone: Gently mix the room-temperature mascarpone cheese into the warm egg mixture until just combined and smooth. Do not overmix, or the cheese may curdle.
3. Whip the Cream: In a separate chilled bowl, whip the heavy cream to stiff peaks using an electric mixer.
4. Fold the Cream: Carefully fold the whipped cream into your mascarpone-egg mixture until smooth, light, and fluffy
5. Assemble the Layers: Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the coffee mixture (just 1 second per side so they don't get soggy) and arrange them in a tight single layer at the bottom of a 9-inch square baking dish. Spoon half of the mascarpone cream over the biscuits and smooth it flat.
6. Repeat: Add a second layer of dipped ladyfingers, followed by the remaining mascarpone cream.Chill & Serve: Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4 hours
