Cookies

Ingredients
- 115g butter, melted½ cup
- (105g) white granulated sugar
- ½ cup (100g) brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1½ cups (210g) standard all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ½ cup chocolate chips or chunks

Instructions

1. Combine wet ingredients: Melt the butter in a microwave-safe jug or small pot on the stove. Pour it into a large mixing bowl, add the white and brown sugars, and whisk thoroughly until combined. Whisk in the egg and vanilla extract until smooth.
2. Add dry ingredients: Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt. Mix gently with a wooden spoon or spatula until a soft dough forms. Fold in the chocolate chips until evenly distributed.
3. scoop the dough: Spoon 12 equal-sized balls onto your prepared baking tray. Space them a few centimetres apart as they will spread while baking.
4. Bake: Bake for 10 to 12 minutes for a soft, chewy center. If you prefer a crunchier biscuit, leave them in for 12 to 14 minutes until the edges turn golden brown.
