Cheesecake

Base: - 180g Graham Crackers or Digestive biscuits
- 65g unsalted butter, melted2 tbsp granulated sugar

Filling:
- 3 blocks (approx. 680g) cream cheese, softened to room temperature
- 200g granulated sugar
- 120g sour cream2 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 large eggs, room temperature
- A pinch of salt

Instructions

preheat & Prep:
1. Heat your oven to 180°C. Lightly grease a 24cm springform pan.
2. Make the Crust: Crush the biscuits into fine crumbs. Mix with the melted butter and 2 tablespoons of sugar until it feels like wet sand. Press the mixture
firmly into the bottom and slightly up the sides of the pan. Bake for 15 minutes, then set aside to cool. 3. Mix the Filling: Beat the room-temperature cream cheese on medium-low speed until completely smooth. Gradually mix in the 200g of sugar, followed by the salt, vanilla extract, and sour cream. Mix just until smooth; do not overbeat.
4. Add the Eggs: Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low speed after each addition just until incorporated. This prevents trapping too much air in the batter, which causes cracks.Bake: Pour the filling over your cooled crust.
5. Bake at 180°C for 15 minutes, then turn the oven down to 150°C and bake for another 30 to 35 minutes. The edges should be set, but the center will still wobble slightly.
6. Cool Slowly: Turn off the oven, leave the door closed, and let the cheesecake sit inside for 1 hour to cool slowly. Move it to a wire rack on the counter for another hour before placing it into the fridge.
7. Chill: Chill in the refrigerator for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight, before releasing it from the springform pan and slicing.
